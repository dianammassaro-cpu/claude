Phase One Inside the Walls

PEP meets people where they are — inside prison, years before release. Character and competency are built before the gate ever opens.

Digital · All of TDCJ
Leadership Academy

PEP's digital character formation experience, available to all 134,000+ people in TDCJ via tablet. Participants work through behavior-based scenarios built around PEP's 10 Driving Values — creating an objective readiness signal before any in-person resource is deployed.

  • 134K+ total addressable population across TDCJ
  • Self-paced — no transfers or waitlists required
  • Feeds directly into Incentivized Housing admissions
In-Prison · Community
Incentivized Housing

TDCJ housing units converted into live learning communities. Each 126-bed pod operates as a micro-campus where participants live, learn, and collaborate daily around PEP's values — led increasingly by alumni Servant Leaders from within.

  • Under $500 per participant per year
  • Reaches individuals 3 years from parole — 80% of TDCJ releases
  • 25% advance into the Core Program as eligibility approaches
Flagship · In-Prison
Core Program

PEP's highest-intensity experience — a Baylor University-recognized entrepreneurship curriculum, Toastmasters training, and executive mentorship culminating in a Shark Tank–style Business Plan Competition. Families attend graduation. Graduates become Servant Leaders for the next cohort.

  • 400+ executive volunteers per year
  • Sub-10% recidivism held for 15+ consecutive years
  • Family engagement and graduation ceremony included
Phase Two The Bridge

Nearly half of returns to prison happen within 90 days of release. PEP's case managers are waiting at the gate.

Post-Release · First 90 Days
Reentry Services

Case managers meet every graduate at release with immediate practical support — transportation, IDs, housing, and parole coordination. Transitional homes in Houston and Dallas reinforce PEP culture. After stabilization, the 12-week eSchool builds professional skills, financial literacy, and entrepreneurial foundations.

  • Case manager meets graduate at the gate — every time
  • Transitional housing in Houston and Dallas, expanding to 200+ beds by 2028
  • eSchool: budgeting, interview skills, resume writing, business literacy

Phase Three Life on the Outside

Transformation is only meaningful if it leads somewhere. PEP converts preparation into businesses, jobs, and generational wealth.

Outside · Entrepreneurship
Post-Release Entrepreneurship

The pathway starts with Aspiration — a 4-week cohort where participants pitch a business idea to executive volunteers. Those who secure a coach advance into Applied Entrepreneurship, a competency-based program with 1-on-1 coaching at PEP hubs in Houston, Dallas, and Austin.

  • Aspiration: market testing and live pitch event
  • Applied Entrepreneurship: mastery-based progression, no calendar clock
  • 500 entrepreneurs served annually by 2030
Financing · CDFI
Entre Capital

The only CDFI in the nation exclusively serving returning citizens. Loans from $2,500 to $200,000 — every one paired with mandatory coaching, reporting requirements, and clear benchmarks. Capital is deployed only after character, competence, and viability have been proven through PEP's full formation pathway.

  • 0% borrower recidivism rate
  • 50 average coaching hours per borrower per year
  • Targeting $25M+ loan portfolio by 2034
