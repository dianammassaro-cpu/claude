What to Expect

Real classroom.
Real stakes. Real results.

PEP runs a structured semester inside TDCJ units. Volunteers are the faculty. You bring your professional expertise; PEP provides training, materials, and full logistics support. The only thing you supply is your time and candor.

Before you arrive
✏️

Orientation & Preparation

PEP provides thorough orientation covering what to expect inside a correctional unit, TDCJ protocols, and how to communicate effectively.

Inside the unit
🤝

Teaching & Mentoring

Teach in small groups, review business plans, judge pitch competitions, or run workshops in your expertise area, from accounting to HR to marketing. Participants are motivated, prepared, and sharp.

After graduation
✓

Ongoing Mentorship

Stay connected post-release as an accountability partner, employer connection, or business advisor. Many of PEP's strongest success stories trace back to a single sustained relationship.

Volunteer Roles

Find your fit.

Not sure where you fit?

Inside the Unit

Business Curriculum Instructor

Teach a module of PEP's MBA-style curriculum: finance, marketing, operations, or strategy. Materials provided. You bring your professional experience and real-world perspective.

Inside the Unit

Business Plan Judge

Evaluate participant business plans at the culminating pitch competition, PEP's equivalent of Demo Day. Provide written feedback and score presentations against real entrepreneurial criteria.

Inside the Unit

Character Formation Facilitator

Lead discussions on leadership, ethics, accountability, and servant leadership. Conversation-driven, not lecture-driven. Your life experience matters more than your credentials.

Post-Release

Reentry Mentor

Walk alongside a graduate in their first 12 months post-release, meeting monthly, making connections, and providing accountability during the critical window when most relapses occur.

Post-Release

Employment Connector

Open your network. Introduce graduates to hiring managers. Host a lunch. Make the call. Even one connection can break the cycle, and you may find your next great employee in the process.

Virtual

Online Workshop Facilitator

Deliver a workshop remotely on resume writing, interview skills, financial literacy, or digital tools. Reach participants across multiple TDCJ units simultaneously.

Common Questions

Good to know before you sign up.

Answers to what most first-time volunteers want to know.

Is it safe?
Yes. PEP operates inside controlled TDCJ facilities with full security staff present. In 20+ years, no volunteer has been harmed. PEP's thorough orientation fully prepares you for the environment.
What if I have a prior record?
Reach out and let us know. PEP reviews every application individually. Having a record does not disqualify you, in many cases, lived experience makes you a more effective teacher.
Do I need to bring anything?
Just yourself and a valid state ID. No electronics, no bags beyond what security allows. PEP provides everything else, including materials, name badges, and an escort through the unit.
Can I bring colleagues?
Yes, corporate groups are welcome and common. PEP can facilitate group volunteer days for teams of 5 to 50. Contact us directly to coordinate.
Can my company sponsor volunteering?
Many PEP corporate partners formalize their relationship, gaining recognition, reporting, and coordinated access. Reach out to discuss a corporate partnership structure.
