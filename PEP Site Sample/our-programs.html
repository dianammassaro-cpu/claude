Our Programs

to purpose.

A continuous, vertically integrated system rooted in servant leadership — beginning with character formation and education inside TDCJ and walking alongside graduates long after release.

The PEP Journey

Three phases. One mission.

PEP meets people where they are — inside prison, at the gate, and years after release. Servant leadership is the thread that runs through every phase: graduates earn their transformation and then give it back, coaching the cohort behind them.

Phase 1 — Inside
Formation + Education
  • Leadership Academy — digital character formation via tablet, available to all 134K+ TDCJ inmates
  • Incentivized In-Prison Housing — peer-led dormitory communities building servant leadership
  • Core Program — Baylor-recognized business education culminating in a Business Plan Competition
  • Graduates become Servant Leaders — coaching the next cohort from within
Phase 2 — Transition
Stabilization
  • Reentry Services — case managers meet graduates at the gate with housing and a plan
  • Transitional Housing — safe, culture-reinforcing homes in Houston and Dallas
  • 12-week life skills and business literacy program in the first 90 days
Phase 3 — Outside
Enterprise
  • 4-week entrepreneurial discernment cohort with executive pitch event
  • Competency-based formation with 1-on-1 coaching
  • Entre Capital — growth financing from $2,500–$200,000 paired with 50 hours of coaching

Phase 3 · Outside · Entrepreneurship

Post-Release
Entrepreneurship

Transformation inside prison is only meaningful if it leads somewhere. PEP's post-release entrepreneurship pathway closes the loop — converting preparation into visible proof that second chances create jobs, businesses, and generational wealth. Participants begin with a 4-week discernment cohort: clarifying a business idea, testing market assumptions, and pitching to executive volunteers at a live event. Those who secure a coach advance into a competency-based formation experience where progression is driven by demonstrated mastery, not calendar time. PEP's physical hubs in Houston and Dallas function as entrepreneurship centers where stabilization, education, mentorship, and capital come together.

Phase 2 & 3 · Reentry · Post-Release

Family
Reunification

47% of the men we serve are parents. When a man is incarcerated, the weight doesn't fall on him alone — it falls on his children, his partner, and his community. PEP walks alongside families throughout the entire journey: before release, at the gate, and long after.

Family is not a secondary concern. It is a primary motivator. Men who maintain strong family connections have better reentry outcomes — and PEP is built around that reality. Family Liaisons at each unit provide direct support, connection, and guidance to the families of participants.

  • Family Liaisons at Lychner and Estes Units provide direct support to families of participants
  • Family orientation sessions help loved ones understand the PEP journey and what to expect at release
  • Reentry plans are built with family stability as a primary metric — not just employment
  • Transitional housing is designed to reunify, not just shelter — prioritizing proximity to family
  • Alumni community becomes an extended family network for men and their households

Phase 3 · Financing · CDFI

Entre
Capital

The only Community Development Financial Institution (CDFI) in the nation exclusively serving returning citizens. Founded in 2019, Entre Capital provides growth financing from $2,500 to $200,000 — but capital is never deployed alone. Every loan is paired with mandatory coaching, reporting requirements, and clear performance benchmarks.
