Our Programs

From prison
to purpose.

A continuous, vertically integrated system rooted in servant leadership, beginning with character formation and education inside TDCJ and walking alongside graduates long after release.

Not a program. A pipeline.

PEP's model is not a collection of standalone services but a single, connected journey. Every phase builds on the last: digital formation unlocks readiness, in-prison programming deepens character, reentry services stabilize the critical first 90 days, and post-release entrepreneurship converts transformation into lasting economic participation.

The PEP Journey

Three phases. One mission.

PEP meets people where they are, whether inside prison, at the gate or years after release. Servant leadership is the thread that runs through every phase: graduates earn their transformation and then give it back, coaching the cohort behind them.

Phase 1 — Inside
Formation + Education
  • Leadership Academy: digital character formation via tablet, available to all 134K+ TDCJ inmates
  • Incentivized In-Prison Housing: peer-led dormitory communities building servant leadership
  • Core Program: Baylor-recognized business education culminating in a Business Plan Competition
  • Graduates become Servant Leaders, coaching the next cohort from within
Phase 2 — Transition
Stabilization
  • Reentry Services: Reentry team meets graduates at the gate with housing and a plan
  • Transitional Housing: safe, culture-reinforcing homes in Houston and Dallas
  • 12-week life skills and business literacy program in the first 90 days
Phase 3 — Outside
Enterprise
  • 4-week entrepreneurial discernment cohort with executive pitch event
  • Competency-based formation with one-on-one coaching.
  • Entre Capital — growth financing from $2,500–$200,000 paired with 50 hours of coaching
Phase One
Formation + Education — Inside the Walls

Leadership
Academy

PEP's digital character formation experience is being delivered through the Edovo tablet platform to every eligible incarcerated individual in TDCJ, regardless of security classification, sentence length, or proximity to discharge. For the first time, PEP can reach all 134,000+ people in the Texas prison system, not just those within three years of release.

Incentivized
In-Prison Housing

As PEP's fastest-growing program and the bridge between digital learning and the flagship Core Program, PEP is converting TDCJ Incentivized Housing units into live learning hubs, bringing community, peer accountability and servant leadership to individuals who were previously unreachable due to sentence length.

Core
Program

PEP's highest-intensity formation experience and the gold standard of criminal justice programming in America, the Core Program combines Toastmasters, a Baylor University-recognized entrepreneurship curriculum and mentorship from executive volunteers. The course culminates in a Shark Tank-style Business Plan Competition judged by real industry leaders. Graduates then transform into Servant Leaders as they pour into the next cohort.

Phase Two
Stabilization — The Bridge

Reentry
Services

Nearly half of all returns to prison happen within the first 90 days of release. Most people walk out with $50 and a bus pass — no housing, no plan. PEP's Reentry Services are designed to meet that moment. Life Caddies, who are case managers with lived experience of the reentry process, meet graduates at the gate. They’ve walked the same road, and that’s what sets PEP apart from other reentry programs.

  • Transitional housing in Houston and Dallas offers a structured, culture-reinforcing environment
  • Education for life skills: budgeting, goal-setting, interviewing, resume writing, and job search skills
Phase Three
Enterprise — Life on the Outside

Post-Release
Entrepreneurship

Transformation inside prison is only meaningful if it leads somewhere. PEP's post-release pathway converts preparation into jobs, businesses, and generational wealth. Participants begin with a four-week discernment cohort to clarify a business idea, test market assumptions and pitch to volunteers. Progression is competency-based, not time-based, with one-on-one coaching at entrepreneurship hubs in Houston and Dallas.

Family
Reunification

Of the men we serve, 47% are parents. Incarceration doesn't just affect the one who's in prison, but also the children, partner and community. Men with strong family connections have better reentry outcomes, and PEP is built around that reality. Family Liaisons at Lychner and Estes Units provide direct support throughout the journey.

47%
of PEP participants are parents
46%
of alumni achieve homeownership
22%
household income drop after parental incarceration — PEP reverses this
  • Family Liaisons at Lychner and Estes Units provide support to families of participants
  • Family members receive updates on their participant so that loved ones better understand the PEP journey and what to expect after graduation.
  • Reentry plans are built with family stability as a primary metric, not just employment
  • Alumni community becomes an extended family network for men and their households
A PEP Wholly-Owned Subsidiary Entre Capital CDFI Community Development Financial Institution

Entre
Capital

Founded in 2019 as the only Community Development Financial Institution (CDFI) in the nation exclusively serving returning citizens, Entre Capital provides growth financing from $2,500 to $200,000. But capital is never deployed alone. Every loan is paired with mandatory coaching, reporting requirements and clear performance benchmarks.
