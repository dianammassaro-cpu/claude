What happens next

Simple process.
Lasting impact.

Not all roles take place inside a prison — some are post-release mentorship, others are virtual or community-based. The next steps depend on what you sign up for. Here’s how it works:

01

You fill out the form

Tell us your background, availability, and which roles interest you. No prior experience required for any of them.

02

We match you to a role

Our volunteer coordinator reaches out within two business days to confirm your fit and explain exactly what your role involves.

03

Preparation (varies by role)

In-prison roles require a TDCJ background check and a brief orientation. Post-release and virtual roles have a lighter onboarding — PEP walks you through whatever’s needed.

04

You get started

First-time in-prison volunteers are paired with an experienced PEP leader. Post-release and virtual volunteers are connected directly with their match or group.

Note on background checks: Required for in-prison roles only. Having a prior record does not automatically disqualify you — PEP reviews each application individually and believes in second chances at every level.
