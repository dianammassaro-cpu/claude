What We Do

A proven model.
Inside and outside the walls.

PEP works in two phases. Inside Texas prisons, we deliver rigorous business education and character formation. Upon release, we surround graduates with housing, employment, mentorship, and capital — so transformation sticks.

The result is measurable: lower recidivism, higher wages, active tax contributions, and businesses that hire others.

Our Model Explained →
01 — Inside

Character Formation

Moral decision-making, accountability, and identity — PEP's peer-driven curriculum transforms how men see themselves and their potential.

02 — Inside

Servant Leadership

Volunteer executives model and teach servant leadership as a core discipline — shaping men who lead through service, integrity, and earned trust.

03 — Inside

Business Education

Finance, marketing, operations, and pitch presentation — culminating in a real Business Plan Competition judged by volunteer executives.

04 — Outside

Reentry Support

Housing, transportation, employment placement, and case management in the critical first 90 days after release.

05 — Outside

Post Release Entrepreneurship

Upon release, graduates enter a dedicated entrepreneurship track — with co-working space, coaching, and capital to launch their businesses and build their futures.

06 — Outside

Entre Capital Acceleration

PEP's Entre Capital fund provides loan capital and hands-on acceleration for graduate entrepreneurs ready to grow — turning business plans into real companies.

The Economic Case

Where your dollar goes to work.

Independent economic analysis shows PEP generates substantial, quantifiable returns for Texas taxpayers — far beyond what it costs to run the program. Every dollar donated is a dollar that multiplies.

$1.92

Avoided Incarceration Costs

For every $1 invested in PEP, Texas avoids $1.92 in incarceration spending alone. Fewer people return to prison; fewer cells fill up.

$8.94

Full Economic Return

When employment tax revenue, avoided social costs, and family stability are included, the full return reaches $8.94 per $1 invested.

$0

State Funding Received

PEP receives no government money. Funded entirely by individuals, corporations, and foundations who believe in measurable results.

Source: Independent economic analyses — Baylor University (2013) and Initiative for a Competitive Inner City (2018). Full methodology available on request.

Breaking It Down

Where the return comes from.

  • $1.92
    Avoided Incarceration Costs
    Texas spends ~$32,000 per incarcerated person per year. PEP's sub-10% recidivism rate means thousands of men never return — saving the state millions annually.
  • +$3.80
    Employment & Tax Revenue
    100% job placement within 90 days. Graduates pay income taxes, sales taxes, and property taxes — becoming contributors, not costs.
  • +$3.22
    Social Cost Reduction
    Stable fathers reduce dependency on public services. Fewer broken families. Lower long-term childhood poverty. Measurable generational benefit.
  • = $8.94
    Total Return Per Dollar
    Each dollar donated to PEP returns $8.94 to Texas — making this one of the highest-ROI investments in criminal justice reform available today.
