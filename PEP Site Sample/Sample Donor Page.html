What We Do

A proven model.
Inside and outside the walls.

PEP works in two phases. Inside Texas prisons, we deliver rigorous business education and character formation. Upon release, we surround graduates with housing, employment, mentorship, and capital — so transformation sticks.

The result is measurable: lower recidivism, higher wages, active tax contributions, and businesses that hire others.

01 — Inside

Character Formation

Moral decision-making, accountability, and identity — PEP's peer-driven curriculum transforms how men see themselves and their potential.

02 — Inside

Servant Leadership

Volunteer executives model and teach servant leadership as a core discipline — shaping men who lead through service, integrity, and earned trust.

03 — Inside

Business Education

Finance, marketing, operations, and pitch presentation — culminating in a real Business Plan Competition judged by volunteer executives.

04 — Outside

Reentry Support

Housing, transportation, employment placement, and case management in the critical first 90 days after release.

05 — Outside

Post Release Entrepreneurship

Upon release, graduates enter a dedicated entrepreneurship track — with co-working space, coaching, and capital to launch their businesses and build their futures.

06 — Outside

Entre Capital Acceleration

PEP's Entre Capital fund provides loan capital and hands-on acceleration for graduate entrepreneurs ready to grow — turning business plans into real companies.

Ways to Give

Give the way that works for you.

  • Online
    One-Time or Monthly Gift
    Donate securely at pep.org/donate. Monthly giving at any level makes you a sustaining partner — and gives PEP the planning stability to maximize every dollar.
  • DAF
    Donor-Advised Fund
    Grant from your DAF to Prison Entrepreneurship Program, EIN 20-0970590. Most major platforms (Fidelity Charitable, Schwab, Vanguard) can process in minutes.
  • Stock
    Appreciated Stock
    Donate shares directly and avoid capital gains tax while receiving a deduction for the full fair-market value. Contact financials@pep.org to initiate a stock transfer to Prison Entrepreneurship Program, EIN 20-0970590.
  • Corp.
    Corporate Sponsorship & Matching
    Sponsor a class, event, or program phase. Many companies match employee gifts dollar-for-dollar — check with HR to multiply your impact without spending more.
  • Estate
    Legacy & Planned Giving
    Leave a lasting mark. Include PEP in your will, trust, or beneficiary designations. Contact financials@pep.org to discuss options.
  • Class
    Sponsor a PEP Class
    Fund a Personal Transformation Memorandum (PTM) class directly — supporting participants through one of PEP’s core curriculum modules. Contact us to discuss class sponsorship recognition and reporting.
  • Mail
    Check by Mail
    Make checks payable to Prison Entrepreneurship Program and mail to:
    P.O. Box 2767, Houston, TX 77252-2767
