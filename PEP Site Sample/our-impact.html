Recidivism in Context

The gap that defines PEP

Recidivism is the most important measure of any criminal justice program. Nationally, 60% of people released from prison are re-arrested within 3 years. In Texas, the rate is 17%. PEP's rate, sustained for over 15 years, is below 10% — and falling.

That gap isn't explained by selection bias alone. PEP participants are drawn from the same Texas prison population. The difference is the model.
