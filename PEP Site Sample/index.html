Changing
lives.
For good.

We unite executives and inmates through entrepreneurial passion and servant leadership to transform lives, restore families and rebuild communities.

The relationship begins inside the walls. The community — and the real test — continues on the outside.

What sets PEP apart is where we start: inside Texas prisons, building genuine relationships and equipping men with the character, skills, and entrepreneurial mindset to succeed. Then we walk it out with them — surrounding graduates with jobs, housing, mentorship, and a community that doesn't disappear at the gate.

The Men We Serve

Inside a Texas prison, something extraordinary happens when a man discovers he has unlimited potential. PEP's program — character formation, entrepreneurship education, and a Business Plan Competition — doesn't just prepare men for release. It changes who they are.

Volunteer

Our ~800 annual executive volunteers aren't just helping — they're essential. The "collision" between a hungry entrepreneur inside and a seasoned leader on the outside is where transformation ignites. Step inside a unit, judge a business plan, or mentor virtually.

For Families

47% of incarcerated men are parents. When a man changes, his family changes. PEP walks alongside families through incarceration and after — offering support, connection, and real tools to rebuild the bonds that matter most.

For Families

Incarceration separates.
PEP restores.

When a man is incarcerated, the weight falls on his whole family. Children grow up without fathers. Partners carry impossible loads. PEP walks alongside families — not just participants — throughout incarceration and into freedom.

  • Support and connection throughout incarceration
  • Reentry planning that centers family reunification
  • Housing and employment that stabilizes the whole household
  • An alumni community that becomes a second family
  • 46% of graduates achieve homeownership
