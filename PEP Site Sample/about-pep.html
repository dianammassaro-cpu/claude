About PEP

Transforming
lives since
2004.

We unite executives and inmates through entrepreneurial passion and servant leadership to transform lives, restore families and rebuild communities.

We don't believe in writing people off. We never have.

Founded in 2004, the Prison Entrepreneurship Program is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit operating inside Texas Department of Criminal Justice units. We build genuine relationships between incarcerated entrepreneurs and business executives — beginning inside the walls, and continuing with a community that walks alongside graduates long after release. The result is one of the most measurably effective criminal justice programs in America.

Our Story

Built on a belief that
every person has potential.

PEP was founded on a simple but radical premise: that the men inside Texas prisons are not problems to be managed — they are potential waiting to be unlocked. What was needed wasn't charity. It was opportunity, accountability, and the belief that transformation is possible for anyone willing to do the work.

Over two decades, we have built a program that operates in two phases. Inside, we deliver rigorous business education, character formation, and servant leadership — taught by real executives who volunteer their time. Men compete in a Business Plan Competition judged by their peers and industry leaders. The standards are high. The results reflect it.

Outside, we don't disappear at the gate. PEP surrounds graduates with housing, employment, mentorship, and capital. Entre Capital provides financing for alumni entrepreneurs. Life Caddies walk alongside graduates through the critical post-release window. An alumni community becomes a second family.

PEP is driven by a broad coalition of individuals, corporations, foundations, and public partners who believe in results — and PEP delivers them.
